### 2. İşlenen veriler

Özelliğe bağlı olarak Discord kullanıcı kimliği, kullanıcı adı ve görünen ad, avatar, hesap oluşturulma bilgisi, üyelik ve roller, sunucu/kanal/mesaj kimlikleri ve işlem tarihleri işlenebilir.

Ayrıca aşağıdaki özellik verileri işlenir:

| --- | --- |

### 3. Staff aktifliği ve diğer oyuncular

Staff/yetkiliyseniz, ekip görev koşulları kapsamında rank değerlendirmesi ve uygun yetkiye yerleştirme amacıyla Discord ses kanallarındaki aktiflik süreniz tutulur. Bu özellik kanal giriş/çıkış olaylarını ve süreleri işler; konuşma sesini kaydetmez.

Normal oyuncuların Discord ses aktifliği bu özellik kapsamında tutulmaz. Yetkililer, kendilerine bağlı aktiflik verilerinin silinmesini e-posta ile talep edebilir.

Ekip performansı için işlem kayıtları yetkililere ilişkindir. Ancak oyuncu kaydı, plaka/donate işlemleri ve sunucu moderasyonu normal oyuncuların verilerini de işleyebilir. Özellikle mevcut etiketleme koruması, ihlali yapan oyuncunun kimliği ve ihlal sayısı gibi kayıtlar oluşturur.

### 4. Mesajlar, ticket ve destek

Bot, Racon Roleplay sunucusunda erişim izni bulunan kanallardaki yeni mesajları otomatik denetim akışlarına alır. Kanal kuralları, format kontrolü, moderasyon ve kayıt işlemleri için mesaj içeriği, fotoğraf/dosya eklerinin varlığı ve ilgili alanlar kontrol edilebilir; uygulanacak işlem kanal ve özelliğe göre değişir. Fotoğraf ve dosyalar bu denetim amacıyla botun veri depolarına indirilip arşivlenmez. Mesajlara erişim ticket mesajlarıyla sınırlı değildir. Ticket konusu için kullanıcı mesajlarındaki anahtar kelimelerden kanal adı oluşturulabilir; bu işlem konuşma arşivi oluşturmaz.

Ticket ve destek konuşmaları bu bot tarafından ayrı bir konuşma arşivi veya transcript olarak veritabanına kaydedilmez. Konuşmaların Discord üzerindeki varlığı, Discord'un ve varsa ayrı ticket uygulamalarının işleyişine bağlıdır.

Destek/ticket işlemleri ekip sayaçlarına katkı sağlar. Mevcut ticket kayıtları yalnızca toplam sayıyı değil, işlemi yapan yetkilinin Discord kimliğini, kanal adını, kapanış zamanını ve mükerrer sayımı önleyen mesaj kimliğini de içerebilir; konuşma metni bu kaydın parçası değildir.

Bazı özelliklerde mesaj kopyaları Discord log kanallarına iletilir. Gereksiz ham mesaj içerikleri konsola kaydedilmez. Gerekli log kanalı kopyaları için kullanıcılar e-posta ile silme talebinde bulunabilir. Bu durum, hiçbir mesajın işlenmediği veya hiçbir kopyasının bulunmadığı anlamına gelmez.

Staff aktiflik ve ekip işlem kayıtları, ilgili rank/ekip yönetimi işlevi için gerekli oldukları sürece tutulur. Kullanıcı silme talebinde bulunabilir. İhtiyaç sona erdiğinde kayıtlar kaldırılır.

### 6. Depolama, erişim ve paylaşım

Bot verileri Discord dışında MongoDB veritabanlarında ve yerel dosyalarda saklanır. İlgili bildirimler ve log mesajları Discord üzerinde de bulunabilir. Donate takibinde Google Apps Script üzerinden alınan kayıtlar kullanılır.

Bot sahibi ve görevleri için gerekli erişime sahip yetkililer ilgili verilere erişebilir. Discord kanallarında gösterilen kayıt ve aktiflik raporlarının görünürlüğü kanal izinlerine bağlıdır; aktiflik sıralamaları ilgili özellik üzerinden paylaşılabilir.

Discord, Google ve barındırma hizmetleri botun çalışması için kendi rolleri kapsamında veri işleyebilir. Veriler reklam amacıyla satılmaz veya reklam ağına aktarılmaz; incelenen uygulamada bu yönde bir özellik yoktur.

## English — Privacy Policy

### 1. Scope and responsibility

Racon Roleplay Bot is designed exclusively for the Racon Roleplay Discord server. It supports player registration, in-game record management, moderation, support workflows and staff activity evaluations.

The operator is **Racon Roleplay**. Privacy, correction, deletion, support and complaint requests can be sent to **raconroleplayiletisim@gmail.com**. The Discord contact is **@polisei**, user ID **546474889419292674**.

The bot is hosted in Türkiye by a Turkish hosting company. External services such as Discord and Google may have different infrastructure locations and processing arrangements.

### 2. Data processed

Depending on the feature, the bot may process Discord user IDs, usernames/display names, avatars, account creation information, membership and roles, server/channel/message IDs and operation timestamps.

| Data | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Steam HEX linked to a Discord ID and change information | Player registration, account linking and duplicate checks |
| Staff voice join/leave information and activity totals | Internal rank evaluation and assignment of appropriate staff permissions |
| Staff operation, role, command usage, support and ticket records | Operation tracking and staff points/counters |
| Moderation events and violation counts | Enforcing server rules and assessing repeated violations |
| In-game vehicle model, plate and colour with the applicant's Discord ID | In-game vehicle/plate tracking |
| Donation type, in-game job, dates/duration and associated Discord ID | Donation tracking and expiry reminders |

In-game plates and donation records are not real-world vehicle registration or payment card details. However, because they are linked to Discord accounts, they are identifiable user data covered by this policy.

### 3. Staff activity and other players

Staff voice activity is retained under staff duty arrangements to support rank evaluation and assignment of appropriate permissions. The feature processes voice channel events and durations; it does not record audio.

Regular players' voice activity is not retained by this feature. Staff may request deletion of their activity data by email.

Staff performance records relate to staff members. However, player registration, plate/donation workflows and server moderation may also process regular players' data. In particular, the current mention protection feature can store the offending player's ID and violation count.

### 4. Messages, tickets and support

The bot routes new messages in accessible channels of the Racon Roleplay server through its automated checks. Message content, the presence of photo/file attachments and relevant fields may be checked for channel rules, formatting, moderation and registrations; actions depend on the channel and feature. Photos and files are not downloaded and archived in the bot storage for these checks. Message access is not limited to ticket messages. Keywords in user messages may be used to name ticket channels; this does not create a conversation archive.

This bot does not store ticket/support conversations in its database as a separate conversation archive or transcript. Messages remaining on Discord depend on Discord and any separate ticket application's operation.

Support/ticket operations contribute to staff counters. Current ticket records may also contain the staff member's Discord ID, channel name, closure timestamp and a message ID preventing duplicate counting. Conversation text is not part of that record.

Some features send message copies to Discord log channels. Unnecessary raw message content is not logged to the console. Users may request deletion of relevant log channel copies by email. This does not mean that no messages are processed or copied.

### 5. Retention and deletion

Steam HEX–Discord links are retained while necessary for player registration and account linking. Leaving the server or the passage of 30 days does not trigger automatic deletion. Users may request deletion by contacting **@polisei — 546474889419292674** on Discord or **raconroleplayiletisim@gmail.com**. Requests are processed promptly after account ownership is verified. Data that is no longer necessary for the stated functionality, or must be deleted under Discord requirements or applicable law, is also deleted.

Staff activity and operation records are retained for as long as they are necessary for rank/staff administration. Users may request deletion. Records are removed when no longer needed.

Plate records are retained while necessary for in-game vehicle tracking; donation records while necessary for in-game entitlement and duration history management. These categories have no predefined fixed expiry period. Users may request deletion by email, and records are removed when no longer needed.

Necessary moderation records and message copies are retained while needed for their respective functions; requests use the same contact method. Unnecessary data is not retained. If a valid legal retention obligation applies, the affected data and reason are explained to the requester.

### 6. Storage, access and sharing

Data is stored outside Discord in MongoDB databases and local files. Notifications/log messages may also remain on Discord. Donation tracking uses records retrieved through Google Apps Script.

The bot owner and staff with access necessary for their duties may access relevant data. Visibility of records and activity reports posted on Discord depends on channel permissions; activity rankings may be shared through the relevant feature.

Donation reminders are sent to the relevant user by DM. A copy is also sent to the designated bot owner/administrator for operational tracking. The user's DM preference does not stop this administrator notification.

Discord, Google and hosting services may process data according to their roles in operating the bot. Data is not sold for advertising or transmitted to advertising networks; no such feature exists in the reviewed implementation.

### 7. Notification preferences

Donation reminder DMs are sent only after the user's explicit acceptance.

- **/donatemesajiptal:** Disables the user's donation reminder DMs.
- **/donatemesajac:** Re-enables those DMs.

Opting out does not delete donation records; deletion requests are handled by email.

DM preferences do not disable automated message processing such as moderation or ticket naming. The current implementation has no per-user setting that disables all message processing.

### 8. User requests

Send information, correction or deletion requests to **raconroleplayiletisim@gmail.com**, or contact **@polisei** through Discord support. Email remains available after leaving the server.

Include your Discord user ID and the scope of the request. Account ownership is verified to prevent unauthorised requests. You are not asked to provide passwords, tokens or login credentials.

Requests are handled without delay after verification. Deletion can affect player account links, applications and rank calculations; relevant effects are explained when necessary. Deletion covers all relevant stores containing data associated with the user.

### 9. Security and limits

Access is limited according to duty requirements. Stored data and existing backup copies are protected by encryption; encryption keys are kept separate from source code with restricted access. Security incidents are investigated, corrective action is taken and applicable notification obligations are fulfilled.

Presence Intent has been removed from the code; online status or gaming activities are not tracked through it. User messages are not used to train AI or machine learning models.

The policy is published in a public, policy-only GitHub repository named **polisei / racon-roleplay-bot-politikaları**. It is accessible through **/gizlilik**, **/privacy** and the Discord information channel.

Significant changes are announced in the server's information/announcement channel. The published document shows its update date. Publication does not imply Discord approval of the bot or this policy.
