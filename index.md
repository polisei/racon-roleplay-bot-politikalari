## English — Privacy Policy

### 1. Scope and responsibility

Racon Roleplay Bot is designed exclusively for the Racon Roleplay Discord server. It supports player registration, in-game record management, moderation, support workflows and staff activity evaluations.

The operator is **Racon Roleplay**. Privacy, correction, deletion, support and complaint requests can be sent to **raconroleplayiletisim@gmail.com**. The Discord contact is **@polisei**, user ID **546474889419292674**.

### 2. Data processed

Depending on the feature, the bot may process Discord user IDs, usernames/display names, avatars, account creation information, membership and roles, server/channel/message IDs and operation timestamps.

| Data | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Steam HEX linked to a Discord ID and change information | Player registration, account linking and duplicate checks |
| Staff voice join/leave information and activity totals | Internal rank evaluation and assignment of appropriate staff permissions |
| Staff operation, role, command usage, support and ticket records | Operation tracking and staff points/counters |
| Moderation events and violation counts | Enforcing server rules and assessing repeated violations |
| In-game vehicle model, plate and colour with the applicant's Discord ID | In-game vehicle/plate tracking |
| Donation type, in-game job, dates/duration and associated Discord ID | Donation tracking and expiry reminders |
| Donation notification preference and last notification date | Respecting the user's choice and preventing repeated reminders on the same day |

In-game plates and donation records are not real-world vehicle registration or payment card details. However, because they are linked to Discord accounts, they are identifiable user data covered by this policy.

### 3. Staff activity and other players

Staff voice activity is retained under staff duty arrangements to support rank evaluation and assignment of appropriate permissions. The feature processes voice channel events and durations; it does not record audio.

Regular players' voice activity is not retained by this feature. Staff may request deletion of their activity data by email.

Staff performance records relate to staff members. However, player registration, plate/donation workflows and server moderation may also process regular players' data. In particular, the current mention protection feature can store the offending player's ID and violation count.

### 4. Messages, tickets and support

The bot routes new messages in accessible channels of the Racon Roleplay server through its automated checks. Message content, the presence of photo/file attachments and relevant fields may be checked for channel rules, formatting, moderation and registrations; actions depend on the channel and feature. Photos and files are not downloaded and archived in the bot storage for these checks. Message access is not limited to ticket messages. Keywords in user messages may be used to name ticket channels; this does not create a conversation archive.

This bot does not keep ticket/support conversations as a separate conversation archive. Messages remaining on Discord depend on Discord and any separate ticket application's operation.

Support/ticket operations contribute to staff points. Records may include the staff member's and support participants' Discord IDs, a short description of the operation, channel information and the operation date. Ticket closure records may contain the closing staff member's ID, channel name, closure timestamp and a message ID preventing duplicate counting. These are not full conversation recordings.

Some features send copies of relevant operational messages to Discord record channels whose visibility depends on channel permissions. Users may request deletion of these copies through the same contact methods.


Entry, exit and permission messages in the formation-permission channel are operational instructions submitted by authorised users to the bot. They support formation/clan/family/group membership and related role management with staff approval. This feature processes relevant channel messages; it does not read private DM conversations.

Relevant operational messages may remain in the Discord channel. The player's Discord ID, formation information, entry/exit operation and date may also be retained. This limited history supports tracking which in-game group a player leaves or joins and enforcing server rules for group changes. It does not create a free-form conversation archive or a profile of real-world social relationships.

Data that is no longer necessary for the stated functionality, or must be deleted under Discord requirements or applicable law, is also deleted.
